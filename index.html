Pay is held for half a month. Back pay = full-month pay due − amount already paid (any 20% commission already paid is deducted). It only tops up and never deducts.

Attendance standard: 80%

Hosts who work the whole period need 12 days per half-month and 24 days per month.

Hosts who join mid-period need 80% of their days on board (start day included), rounded to the nearest day. Example: start on the 10th, 6 days on board by the 15th, 5 days required.

Once attendance is met, every day streamed is paid. 12 days streamed pays 12 days. 15 days streamed pays 15 days.

Grounds for appeal

A host with fewer than 12 days can appeal to Haru on any of these grounds:

  1. Illness that needs a long leave to rest
  2. A family emergency that needs a long leave
  3. Not in good condition and asking for rest to recover
  4. Repeated unexpected events (emergencies, sudden incidents)
  5. Other situations that need a specific explanation

Limits: long sick leave (1), long family leave (2) and rest to recover (3) all count as long leave. A host gets one long leave in total every three months. They cannot be combined, and further requests are not approved. Grounds 4 and 5 have no fixed limit and are judged case by case.

Video proof for an appeal

  1. Fever: film the thermometer reading with your face in the shot
  2. Film yourself taking the related medicine
  3. Other illness: film the medicine and yourself swallowing it
  4. Personal matters: film the situation and name a witness

How the 20% commission works

20% commission = diamonds ÷ 100 × 50% × 20% × the USD to PHP rate. The calculator on this page uses a fixed rate of ₱59 as an estimate. It is for reference only, and the amount actually credited in the backend prevails.
